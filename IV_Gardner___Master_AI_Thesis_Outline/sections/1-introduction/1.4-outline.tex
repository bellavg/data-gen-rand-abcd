\section{Thesis Outline}
\label{sec:intro:outline}
Chapter~\ref{sec:related_work} introduces the necessary background (logic synthesis and
AIGs, graph neural networks, and the three families of graph reduction) and then reviews
the literature the gap in~\ref{sec:intro:gap} sits in. Chapter~\ref{sec:methodology}
describes the encoder, the reduction methods implemented for each family, the dataset and
its labels, and the experimental protocol. Chapter~\ref{sec:results} reports results
per research question. Chapter~\ref{sec:discussion} interprets them against the literature
and states the limitations, and Chapter~\ref{sec:conclusion} answers each research question
in turn and outlines future work.
